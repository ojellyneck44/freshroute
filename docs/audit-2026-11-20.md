# FreshRoute audit, 20 Nov 2026

**Scope:** the live site (freshroute.pages.dev), WebKit at iPad Pro 11 landscape (1194 × 834). It covers nine screens:

- Welcome
- Setup
- Plan pickups
- Store detail
- Before you drive
- Trucks
- Reports
- Stores
- Store link

**Method:** the ux-ui-audit probes (core, heuristics, perception, LTR), screenshots and a reading pass. Accessibility findings are judged against WCAG 2.2. Usability findings are judged against Nielsen's heuristics. Typography is judged against DESIGN.md and Apple's text styles.

**Not covered:** the navigation screen during a live drive and the report screen. Both were checked by hand in the simulator, not probed. Screen reader testing with VoiceOver was not done.

## Findings and fixes

### Accessibility

1. **Map credits were unreadable.** MapLibre's attribution text was `rgba(0,0,0,0.75)` on `rgb(35,37,43)`, a ratio of 1.3:1. SC 1.4.3 requires 4.5:1. *Fixed:* the credits now use `--fr-text-2` on `--fr-raised`.
2. **Headings skipped a level.** The heading levels jumped from h1 to h3 on Setup ("Recording during drives"), Store detail ("What they usually donate") and Trucks ("No trucks on the road"). This breaks SC 1.3.1 heading structure. *Fixed:* these are now h2, with the same visual style.
3. **The expired store link had no h1.** Its only text was a 14 px error banner at 4.3:1. *Fixed:* the page now has an h1, "That pickup link doesn't work", and body text with a next step.
4. **The name field used `autocomplete="given-name"`** but asks for first and last name (SC 1.3.5). *Fixed:* `name`.

### Usability (Nielsen)

5. **Visibility of system status.** No map said what it shows (user report: "the map is confusing everywhere"). *Fixed:* every map now has a key that names its purpose and every symbol:
   - "Stores donating food"
   - "Where each truck is now"
   - "This truck's trip"
   - "Where this drive went"
   - "Your drive"
6. **Match with the real world.** "Promise vs forecast" used internal vocabulary. *Fixed:* the heading now reads "Did the truck stay on schedule?", with a one-paragraph explanation, axis captions in words, and "Back vs promised time" for the metric.
7. **Error prevention and recovery.** Location failures other than "denied" showed no message, so the check silently reset. *Fixed:* there is now a message for each case:
   - Wi-Fi-only iPads;
   - the simulator, where it says to pick a place under Features > Location;
   - the Settings path for both Safari and the Home Screen app.
8. **User control.** "Add to route" left the driver on the store page. *Fixed:* it returns to the store list.
9. **Missed turns had no recovery.** The app only said "Head back to the highlighted route". *Fixed:* after 12 seconds off the route, it plans a new route from the van's position. The new route is logged on the drive timeline.
10. **Demo dead end.** At most hours every store was grey, so a demo couldn't start. *Fixed:* five demo stores, spread around Denton, are always open and labelled "Demo store".

### Typography and hierarchy

11. **The sheet title "Pickups" was 26 px against 14 px body** (ratio 1.86). The name label above it was 13 px grey and sat in the iPadOS status bar blur. *Fixed:*
    - Type scale aligned with Apple's text styles: subheadline 14 → 15, title2 24 → 22, title1 26 → 28.
    - The label is now 15 px.
    - Everything at the top of the screen starts below the status bar plus 16 px, through the `--top-safe` token. This also fixes the blurred "Sam · Van 2" and "Back".
12. **Store names in Overpass Bold at 19 px tracked loose.** *Fixed:* −0.01em letter-spacing, matching the headings.
13. **Setup form spacing.** All 7 blocks sat 20 px apart, so nothing grouped (Gestalt proximity). *Fixed:* sections now get extra space above them.
14. **Open:** 42 to 48% of the text on Trucks and Reports is semibold or heavier, so weight has stopped carrying hierarchy. Left for the refocus, because those screens are being redesigned.

### SEO

15. Title, description, canonical, Open Graph, the Twitter card and WebApplication JSON-LD are all present. robots.txt allows only `/`, and the sitemap lists `/`.
16. **Private pickup links could be indexed if shared publicly.** robots.txt blocks crawling but not indexing. *Fixed:* `/s/*` now sends `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow` and `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer` (verified with `curl -I`).
