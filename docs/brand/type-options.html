Primary typeface: five traditional options

Bricolage Grotesque is out. Each option below would replace it for headlines, store names and the wordmark. The interface text stays in the system font (SF Pro on Apple devices), and timecodes stay in IBM Plex Mono. Same sample in every row so you can compare like for like.

A · Overpass
My pick
Keep the promise.Kroger · S Loop 288

Based on Highway Gothic, the lettering on US road signs, which was drawn to be read at speed from a distance.

B · IBM Plex Sans
Keep the promise.Kroger · S Loop 288

Engineered, sober, very trustworthy. Same family as the Plex Mono we already use for evidence.

C · Libre Franklin
Keep the promise.Kroger · S Loop 288

A revival of Franklin Gothic, the classic American newspaper and signage sans. Sturdy and familiar.

D · Source Sans 3
Keep the promise.Kroger · S Loop 288

Adobe's humanist sans, made for interfaces. Friendly and quiet; the least distinctive of the five.

E · SF Pro everywhere
Keep the promise.Kroger · S Loop 288

The most Apple-native: one family for everything. Personality has to come from colour and the icon alone.
